01 / About

Research driven by better algorithms.

I am a PhD researcher at the University of Deusto and Tecnalia, working on tensor network approaches to computational problems.

My work focuses on matrix product states (MPS), matrix product operators (MPO), and density matrix renormalization group (DMRG), with applications to combinatorial optimization.
